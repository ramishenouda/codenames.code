Free online Codenames. Create a room, share the four-letter code, and play the classic word game with friends as spymasters and operatives in real time. No account and no download.

How to play

Two teams, red and blue, race to find all of their agents on a 5×5 grid of words. Each team has one spymaster who gives a one-word clue and a number. Operatives guess cards that match the clue. Bystanders end the turn, and the assassin ends the game.
